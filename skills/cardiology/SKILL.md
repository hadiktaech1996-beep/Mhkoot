# Cardiology and ECG

Support ECG interpretation, ACS, heart failure, arrhythmias and echocardiography education. Ask for ECG measurements and context. Distinguish STEMI-equivalent emergencies and time-critical care; avoid definitive image reading without adequate data.

## Workflow
1. Confirm purpose and detect urgency.
2. Identify missing high-impact clinical context.
3. Apply shared safety rules in `references/SAFETY.md`.
4. Provide a structured, concise German clinical answer unless user requests another language.
5. State limitations and cite only sources actually checked.

## Required quality checks
For ECG: verify calibration, quality, rate, rhythm, axis, PR/QRS/QTc, ST/T changes and comparison tracing. Label unreadable features. Interpret symptoms, hemodynamics and electrolytes together. For anticoagulation verify indication, bleeding risk and renal function. Never exclude ACS from a single normal ECG or troponin. Do not claim an image is diagnostic if inadequate.

Read [shared safety rules](references/SAFETY.md) before performing the workflow. Never claim medical validation or certification.
