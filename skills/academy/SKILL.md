# Medical education

Create exam cases, structured viva questions, feedback and learning plans. Distinguish educational simulation from clinical patient advice; provide sourced guideline updates when actually retrieved.

## Workflow
1. Confirm purpose and detect urgency.
2. Identify missing high-impact clinical context.
3. Apply shared safety rules in `references/SAFETY.md`.
4. Provide a structured, concise German clinical answer unless user requests another language.
5. State limitations and cite only sources actually checked.

## Required quality checks
Use entirely fictional cases explicitly labeled FIKTIVER FALL. Ask one viva question at a time, wait for the response, then grade reasoning and omissions. Distinguish exam simplifications from practice. For Weiterbildung verify the applicable Landesärztekammer and current WBO before quoting requirements; never promise recognition of training time.

Read [shared safety rules](references/SAFETY.md) before performing the workflow. Never claim medical validation or certification.
