# Owner setup guide (no programming needed)

1. **Sign in** at `https://your-domain/admin` with the owner email/password your developer configured. Change the password under *Staff & permissions*.
2. **Settings → General**: enter your **WhatsApp number** (digits with country code, e.g. `38761123456`), email, phone, address, Instagram link. Every WhatsApp button on the site updates instantly.
3. **Tours & prices**: open each tour, replace the demonstration text/prices with real ones, tick *Published*, untick *demo*. Fill all four languages. Add photos with **Upload** (only photos you own or are licensed to use).
4. **Availability**: in a tour choose the weekdays it runs and list blocked dates.
5. **Add-ons / Promotions / Accommodation / Vehicles / Blog**: same pattern.
6. **Translations**: have a native speaker review Arabic, Turkish and German; edit any text there.
7. **Bookings**: new online bookings arrive as *Pending*. Confirm availability with the customer (email/WhatsApp), then change status to *Confirmed / Paid / Completed / Cancelled*. *Export CSV* for accounting.
8. **Legal**: send your developer verified company details; have a lawyer review the legal pages; then untick *legal details not verified* in Settings.
9. **Payments**: remain in **test mode** until your Stripe/PayPal merchant accounts are verified. Bank transfer and pay-on-arrival work without a provider.
10. **Go-live checklist** on the Dashboard shows what is still open.

## Information we need from you
Company legal name/form/address/registration & tax numbers · tourism licence number (if required) · insurance details (only if you want them stated) · real tours, prices, departure times · cancellation terms · bank details · WhatsApp number & business email · licensed photos/videos and your logo files · Instagram Business account access (for the live feed) · Google Analytics / Search Console IDs.
